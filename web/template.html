RESEARCH DESIGN

Methodology

Walk-forward sizing

At each interval start, fit spot price changes on futures price changes from the previous 12, 24 or 36 intervals. Clip the OLS slope to [0, 1.5] and apply it to the following interval. Each hedge starts and closes within one interval.

Net hedge P&L

Spot change minus the short ratio times the futures change and assumed round-trip cost. Volatility is sample standard deviation. The tail metric averages the lowest ceiling(5% × n) monthly P&L observations.

Data and limits

Results omit margin funding, integer lot rounding and market impact. There is no claim of statistical significance or investable returns.
